## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, high-jewelry luxury experience rooted in the craft traditions and high-altitude luminosity of Cusco, Peru. The visual narrative reconciles ancient artisanal resonance with contemporary editorial curation—evoking the weight of carved Andean stone, the iridescence of the Morpho butterfly wing, and the quiet opulence of boutique jewelry ateliers.

The target audience comprises discerning collectors, global luxury travelers, and patrons of fine craftsmanship who value origin, material provenance, and deliberate restraint over fast fashion. The interface must evoke reverence, serenity, tactile warmth, and effortless prestige.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on:
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Expansive, breathing layouts governed by asymmetrical editorial pacing and disciplined typographic hierarchy.
- **Organic Tactility:** Surface interactions that simulate touch, mineral warmth, and delicate physical craft rather than synthetic digital conventions.
- **Architectural Restraint:** Crisp, hairline structural dividers, low-contrast containment, and disciplined geometry honoring Incan stonework without overt pastiche.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around natural Peruvian light, raw mineral pigments, and Andean textiles:

- **Primary Brand (`#C9D9E8` / Celeste Pastel):** Represents the fleeting iridescence of the high-altitude Morpho butterfly and Andean morning skies. Used for luminous focal accents, subtle badges, and curated promotional blocks.
- **Secondary Warm (`#E6D8C3` / Beige Andino):** Evokes tactile river-smoothed stone, adobe, and natural alpaca wool. Deployed across container backdrops, banner panels, and editorial cards.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#B87D3B` / Ocre Cálido):** Warm metallic gold tone, dedicated to precious metal markers, artisanal provenance badges, and ratings.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1E1B18` / Carbón Profundo):** Replaces pure digital black with a deep, mineral-infused charcoal for primary headlines, typography, sharp interactive outlines, and high-impact action buttons.
- **Background Base (`#F8F2E4` / Crema Luminoso):** A warm, unbleached handmade paper tone that serves as the global canvas, mitigating blue-light glare and enriching warm gemstone photography.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#FFFFFF` / Blanco Oficial Puro):** Pure white reserved exclusively for elevated product cards, popovers, select fields, and modal drawers to achieve crisp gemstone color fidelity.
- **Secondary Neutral (`#5C554E` / Taupe Piedra):** Subdued stone gray for metadata, captions, secondary navigation, and micro-labels.
- **Functional System Tones:** Verde Salvia (`#3B6E52`) for in-stock and sustainability notices; Terracota Suave (`#9E3E3E`) for alert states and stock scarcity warnings.

### Distribution Balance
Adhere strictly to a 60-30-10 composition ratio:
- **60% Base Luminosity:** Crema Luminoso (`#F8F2E4`) canvas alongside pure Blanco (`#FFFFFF`) presentation planes.
- **30% Textured Tonals:** Beige Andino (`#E6D8C3`) panels, hairline dividers (`#E6D8C3`), and atmospheric Celeste Pastel (`#C9D9E8`) overlays.
- **10% High-Contrast Anchor:** Carbón Profundo (`#1E1B18`) typography, core action buttons, and fine iconography.

## Typography

Typography balances romantic editorial storytelling with modernist precision:

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in **Noto Serif** (400, including delicate italic flourishes for artisan narratives and collection titles). Its sculpted serifs and high stroke contrast recall classical engraving and luxury print publication.
- **Body, UI, & Navigation:** Set in **Montserrat** (300 for long editorial passages, 400 for standard UI, 500/600 for technical labels and pricing). Its geometric purity brings architectural modernism, offsetting the warmth of the serif.
- **Editorial Micro-Caps:** Category labels, material provenance tags, and secondary action prompts utilize `label-caps`—all uppercase, tracked with deliberate letter spacing (`0.15em`) to emphasize curated craftsmanship.
- **Price Display:** Pricing is never loud or discount-driven. It relies on clean, disciplined weights of Montserrat with soft spacing to maintain understated luxury.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors high-end print design through generous margins, deliberate white space, and an editorial rhythm that elevates product photography.

- **Desktop Grid:** 12-column flexible grid capped at a maximum container width of `1440px`. Column gutters remain at `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer margins of `4rem` (`64px`) to frame jewelry showcases like gallery exhibits.
- **Tablet Grid (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile Grid (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins. Full-bleed edge treatments are permitted solely for collection hero imagery.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Section margins rely on generous breathing room (`space-xl` and above) to prevent cognitive density and maintain an unhurried, serene browsing cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile tonal layers and subtle organic ambient shadows rather than synthetic digital elevation:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Foundation):* Crema Luminoso (`#F8F2E4`) forms the ambient foundational canvas.
  - *Tier 1 (Tactile Zones):* Beige Andino (`#E6D8C3`) panels delineate narrative curation, artisan quotes, and editorial collections.
  - *Tier 2 (Elevated Planes):* Blanco Puro (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces showcase product assets, checkout panels, and flyout cart surfaces.
- **Ambient Shadow System:**
  - *Subtle Card & Menu Float:* `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.03);`
  - *Drawer & Modal Depth:* `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.04);`
- **Architectural Hairlines:** Surfaces utilize 1px solid borders tinted in natural stone (`rgba(92, 85, 78, 0.12)`) or warm sand (`rgba(230, 216, 195, 0.6)`), preserving geometric clarity.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates soft architectural radii:

- **Radius Scale:** Built upon Level `1` (Soft). Standard interactive elements feature `0.25rem` (`4px`) roundedness to reflect hand-chiselled stone contours rather than synthetic, bubbly curves.
- **Product Cards & Floating Panels:** Employ `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii, providing a refined, gentle frame that respects high-resolution artisanal photography.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** Constrained between `2px` and `4px` to preserve sharp tailored silhouettes.
- **Pills & Circular Badges:** Reserved solely for micro-indicators (such as gemstone carat tags and color swatch selections).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Carbón Solid):** Background `#1E1B18`, text `#F8F2E4`, `border-radius: 3px`. Font: Montserrat, 12px, 600 weight, uppercase with `0.12em` letter spacing. Padding: `16px 32px`. Hover state softly lightens to Taupe Piedra (`#3A342E`).
- **Secondary Brand (Celeste Luminous):** Background `#C9D9E8`, text `#1E1B18`, hairline border `1px solid rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.08)`. Hover introduces a warm ivory highlight.
- **Tertiary / Editorial Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #1E1B18`, text `#1E1B18`. Hover state fills with `#1E1B18` and transitions text to `#F8F2E4`.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Artifact Showcase Card:** Base `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(230, 216, 195, 0.5)`, `border-radius: 6px`. Image container framed in `4:5` ratio with a soft Crema Luminoso backdrop (`#F8F2E4`). Typography within includes collection name in `label-caps` (`#5C554E`), title in `headline-sm` (`#1E1B18`), and price in `price-sm`.
- **Artisan Narrative Card:** Flat surface in Beige Andino (`#E6D8C3`) without border, carrying rich editorial photography, hand-cut stone iconography, and italicized serif captions.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E6D8C3`, `border-radius: 3px`. Typography in Montserrat 14px. Focus state transitions the border to Carbón Profundo (`#1E1B18`) with no vibrant glow rings.
- **Floating Labels / Placeholders:** Styled in Taupe Piedra (`#5C554E`), 12px uppercase.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hairline `1.5px` border in `#1E1B18`. Checkboxes use sharp inner fills; radio buttons feature a delicate concentric stone point.
- **Material & Gemstone Swatches:** Circular disks (`28px`) bordered with a `1px` offset ring in `#1E1B18` upon selection, presenting physical textures of 18k Andean Gold, 950 Silver, and Chrysocolla.

### Badges & Chips
- **Provenance Badges:** Background `#E6D8C3`, text `#1E1B18`, font Montserrat 10px uppercase, `border-radius: 2px`, padding `4px 10px`.
- **Lapis & Butterfly Accents:** Background `#C9D9E8`, text `#1E1B18`, communicating certified sustainable artisan origin.

### Iconography
- Handcrafted line icons rendered exclusively in a unified `1.5px` stroke weight, reflecting fine filigree wirework. Icon color matches adjacent typography (`#1E1B18` or `#5C554E`).
